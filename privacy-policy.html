Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Clear & Current ("the app") is a personal productivity tool for running a weekly review of your systems, projects, and obligations, based on the GTD (Getting Things Done) methodology. Neither this app nor its methodology is the official product of David Allen Company nor its affiliates. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. It is published by JPL Advisory ("we", "us").

The app works fully offline, with no account required

You can use every core feature of the app — the guided weekly checklist, the Quick Tally, and your history — without ever creating an account. In that mode, your data is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us.

Information you provide if you create an account

Creating an account is optional, and only needed if you want your review history backed up and synced across your own devices, or want AI-generated insights. If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and password, to create and secure your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text — authentication is handled by our infrastructure provider, Supabase, using industry-standard hashing.
  • Your review data: which checklist items you've completed, the labels you give your own inboxes/lists/calendars, your Quick Tally counts, review dates, and time spent reviewing. The app does not have a reflection-notes or journaling feature — there's no free-form personal writing for us to collect in the first place.
  • Your app preferences: your chosen weekly review window and reminder day/time.
  • Push notification delivery details, only if you turn on the weekly reminder in a web browser: a push subscription endpoint and encryption keys used solely to deliver that reminder notification to your browser. (On iOS/Android, the reminder is scheduled entirely on your device using local notifications, and no equivalent data is sent to us.)

AI-generated insights (optional)

If you use the Insights feature, a summary of your review activity — completion counts and Quick Tally counts — is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a short written summary, which is then stored in our database tied to your account. This only happens if you're signed in and you or a scheduled process trigger the feature; it never happens for an account that hasn't opted into cloud sync.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We don't run third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and we don't use your data for tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

How your information is used

Solely to operate the features described above: syncing your review data across your devices, sending your weekly reminder, and generating AI insights. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to operate the app, each of which processes data only on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication.
  • Google (Gemini API) — processes review data to generate AI insight summaries, only when that feature is used.
  • Push notification services (e.g. Apple, Google/Mozilla push infrastructure) — deliver the weekly reminder notification you've opted into, using only the subscription details needed to route that notification to your device or browser.

We do not otherwise share, rent, or sell your information to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Your synced review data is kept for as long as your account exists. AI-generated insights are kept for a limited time and then deleted automatically: weekly and monthly insight summaries are deleted after 2 months, and on-demand "Instant" insights after 10 days. You can also:

  • Delete an individual week's review data at any time from within the app.
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated data from our servers at any time, from within the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This immediately and irreversibly deletes your account record, review history, preferences, and any AI-generated insights from our systems. Data already stored locally on a device you've used the app on is not automatically erased by this — you can clear that separately by uninstalling the app or clearing its local storage.
  • Alternatively, email info@jpladv.com to request deletion.

Security

Data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Our database uses row-level security so that your data is only ever readable by your own account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your information.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 years old. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
